## TL;DR

HQ received 70 submissions for the $50K regional marketing fund. Rather than splitting the money across many small local spends, they are putting the full fund behind a **controlled direct-mail test in three markets** — Michigan, Central Virginia, and New England — with the goal of producing a repeatable lead-generation playbook for the whole network. Selected markets must **match the fund's investment** in their own market. Results are measured against a 60-day baseline of revenue and booked appointments.

## Background

- HQ opened a broad ask: what would move the needle in your market with support from a $50K regional fund, and why does your market need it.
- 70 submissions came in, ranging from well-built plans with real tracking to local event sponsorships and one-off ideas.
- Every submission was scored on a consistent framework: return potential, reach and impact, trackability and measurability, fit to the core patient, market need, and market operational strength. The marketing FAC subcommittee reviewed the pool across multiple sessions.

## Key insight

What markets need most right now is not more of any single tactic or local event — it's **leads in the door and new channels to bring those leads in**. That insight reshaped how the fund is being used.

## The program: market-wide direct-mail test

- **Channel:** Direct mail — new to the network at true market-wide scale, and cleanly trackable back to the source. Reaches prospects who don't convert through digital alone.
- **Digital layering:** The mailing list feeds into digital retargeting so the same households get multiple touchpoints.
- **Creative testing:** Multiple offers and creatives will be tested. One creative set intentionally speaks to women as household decision-makers — often the person opening the mail and nudging the men in their lives to book.

## Selected markets and why

Markets were chosen for **operational excellence** (so results reflect the channel, not the operations), deliberately spanning different points on the performance curve:

| Market | Position | Test question |
|---|---|---|
| Michigan | One of the stronger markets | Can a new channel unlock the next gear of growth in an already high-performing market? |
| Central Virginia | Solid market ready for its next phase of growth | Can this channel fuel momentum and performance? |
| New England | Earlier in its growth curve; clinics need a catalyst | Can this channel give them the push to accelerate? |

## Shared investment requirement

Each of the three markets must **match the fund's investment** in their own market. Framed not as cost-sharing but as ensuring participating clinics are genuinely invested — skin in the game keeps the test disciplined and the results trustworthy.

## Measurement

- Baseline each clinic on **revenue and booked appointments** before anything mails.
- Measure against that baseline over the following **60 days**.
- If the channel proves out, the network gets both the results and a repeatable playbook.

## For markets not selected

- Not a verdict on the submission — this round was a controlled channel test, not a market-by-market grant.
- Submissions are informing how HQ thinks about lead generation going forward; more opportunities will come as what works is expanded.
- If direct mail delivers, clinics outside the test are positioned to benefit next.

## Next steps (selected markets/clinics)

HQ will follow up to discuss the shared investment, timelines, and expectations.
