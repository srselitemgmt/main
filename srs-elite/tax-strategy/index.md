## The plan in five sentences

1. **Defer everything possible now** at a 35–45% effective rate — including the SALT torpedo band between $505K and $606K MAGI, where the true marginal rate is 45.5%.
2. **Build a self-managed short-term rental portfolio** with cost segregation, because STRs are the only real estate structure whose losses offset W-2 income while holding a full-time job.
3. **Relocate to a no-income-tax state before 65** — federal law then bars the former state from ever taxing our retirement distributions.
4. **Convert traditional → Roth hard from 65 to 75**, sized to stop just below an IRMAA cliff, funded with taxable-account cash, while Social Security is delayed to 70.
5. **Never sell what gets a basis step-up** — convert the IRA (no step-up), hold the brokerage and the real estate (step-up erases all depreciation recapture).

## Highest-leverage open questions

Ordered by how much they change the numbers. Full list in [[tax-mitigation-master-plan#Open items to resolve]].

1. **Can either spouse qualify for Real Estate Professional Status?** If yes, *all* rental losses become usable, not just STRs — this would reshape the real estate strategy entirely.
2. **Does the 401(k) allow after-tax contributions + in-plan Roth conversion?** Determines whether the mega backdoor Roth is available.
3. **Does the plan offer Roth catch-up?** From 2026 it is mandatory for high earners — if the plan lacks the feature, $8,000/person/year is forfeited outright.
4. **Any non-qualified deferred comp?** The payout election must be 10+ years to escape former-state tax. Time-sensitive.
5. **Current balances by bucket** — needed to choose between the slow and fast conversion lanes.
