Creami Cookbook

Healthy Ninja Creami pints we actually make. Photos get added as each one comes out of the freezer.

Every recipe here is written for the standard 16 oz pint and filled to the MAX line. Open a recipe and switch it to 2 or 3 pints when you're prepping a batch of containers at once.

Macros are estimates per pint, before mix-ins, and shift with the milk and sweetener you use.

House rules

  1. Freeze 24 hours on a flat, level shelf.
  2. Let the pint sit 5 to 10 minutes before spinning.
  3. Crumbly after the first spin is normal. Add 1 tbsp milk, Re-spin.
  4. Mix-ins go in after the first spin, never before.
