# Ninja Creami Recipes

A working collection of Ninja Creami pint recipes, plus the technique notes that make the difference between a crumbly mess and scoop-shop texture. All recipes are sized for a standard **16 oz pint** (fill to the MAX line, never above). If you have the **Deluxe (24 oz pint)**, scale everything by 1.5x.

## TL;DR

- Freeze pints **24 hours** on a **flat, level** surface. Tilted pints spin unevenly and can wreck the blade.
- Almost every batch needs a **Re-spin**. If it comes out crumbly or powdery, add 1 tablespoon of liquid and Re-spin. Repeat once if needed.
- Texture comes from **fat, sugar, and a stabilizer**, not from the machine. Low-fat, low-sugar bases need help: a tablespoon of instant pudding mix, a bit of cream cheese, or a little xanthan gum.
- Only use the **Mix-in** function for add-ins. Process the base first, dig a hole in the center, add mix-ins, then run Mix-in. Do not add chunks before the first spin.
- Let a rock-hard pint sit on the counter **5 to 10 minutes** before spinning. It spins better and is kinder to the machine.

## Settings cheat sheet

| Setting | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Ice Cream | Full-fat dairy bases with sugar |
| Lite Ice Cream | Protein, low-sugar, or sugar-substitute bases |
| Gelato | Egg-based or very rich bases |
| Sorbet | Fruit plus sugar or juice, no dairy |
| Milkshake | Already-frozen ice cream plus milk |
| Mix-in | Folding in chunks after the first spin |
| Re-spin | Second pass when the first comes out crumbly |

Deluxe-only settings: Creamiflavor (adds flavor evenly through the pint), Frozen Yogurt, Slushi, Italian Ice, and Frozen Drink.

## Classic (full-fat) bases

### Vanilla bean

The reference recipe. Nail this and everything else is a variation.

- 1 tbsp cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup whole milk

1. Microwave the cream cheese 10 seconds. Whisk in the sugar and vanilla until it looks like frosting.
2. Slowly whisk in the cream, then the milk, until smooth.
3. Pour into the pint, freeze 24 hours, spin on **Ice Cream**. Re-spin if needed.

### Chocolate

- 1 tbsp cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup whole milk

Same method as vanilla. Whisk the cocoa into the cream cheese and sugar paste before adding liquid so it doesn't clump. Spin on **Ice Cream**.

### Strawberry cheesecake

- 2 tbsp cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 3/4 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh strawberries
- Mix-in: 1/4 cup crushed graham crackers

Blend everything except the graham crackers until smooth. Freeze and spin on **Ice Cream**. Add the graham crackers and run **Mix-in**.

### Cookies and cream

Make the vanilla base. After spinning, add 4 to 5 roughly chopped chocolate sandwich cookies and run **Mix-in**.

### Mint chip

Make the vanilla base with 1/2 tsp peppermint extract in place of 1/2 tsp of the vanilla. Optional drop of green food coloring. After spinning, add 1/4 cup mini chocolate chips and run **Mix-in**.

### Coffee

Make the vanilla base and whisk 1 tbsp instant espresso powder into the warm cream cheese paste. Spin on **Ice Cream**.

### Peanut butter cup

- 1 tbsp cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 3/4 cup whole milk
- Mix-in: 4 chopped mini peanut butter cups

Whisk the peanut butter into the cream cheese paste, then add liquids. Spin on **Ice Cream**, add the chopped cups, run **Mix-in**.

## High-protein (low-sugar) bases

These are the recipes most people buy a Creami for. They are built from protein shakes or Greek yogurt and depend on a stabilizer for texture. Without one they spin into powder.

### The basic protein pint

Roughly 250 to 300 calories and 40 to 45 g protein per pint, depending on the shake.

- 1 bottle (11 oz) ready-to-drink protein shake, any flavor
- 1 tbsp sugar-free instant pudding mix (vanilla or cheesecake for most flavors, chocolate for chocolate)
- 1 to 2 tbsp sweetener of choice to taste (optional)
- Top off with a splash of milk or almond milk to reach the MAX line

1. Pour the shake into the pint. Whisk in the pudding mix until no lumps remain.
2. Add milk to the MAX line, stir, freeze 24 hours.
3. Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**. Add 1 tbsp milk and **Re-spin**. Expect to Re-spin once more.

Flavor builds on this base:

- **Chocolate peanut butter:** chocolate shake, chocolate pudding mix, 1 tbsp powdered peanut butter. Mix-in: chopped peanuts.
- **Cookies and cream:** vanilla shake, cheesecake pudding mix. Mix-in: 2 crushed sandwich cookies.
- **Birthday cake:** vanilla shake, vanilla pudding mix, 1/2 tsp butter extract. Mix-in: 1 tbsp sprinkles.
- **Salted caramel:** caramel or vanilla shake, butterscotch pudding mix, pinch of flaky salt.

### Pumpkin pie protein

Built from pantry staples: pumpkin purée, vanilla whey, Fairlife milk. The pumpkin adds enough body that no pudding mix is required, though 1 tbsp per pint makes it creamier. About 310 calories and 41 g protein per pint with 2% Fairlife and no added sweetener.

Per pint:

- 1 1/4 cups Fairlife milk, plus a splash to top off to the MAX line
- 1/2 cup pumpkin purée (not pie filling)
- 1 scoop (about 30 g) vanilla whey protein
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 1 to 2 tbsp sweetener to taste (maple syrup, honey, or zero-calorie)

Batch for three pints:

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fairlife milk | 3 3/4 cups, plus a little to top off |
| Pumpkin purée | 1 1/2 cups (most of one 15 oz can) |
| Vanilla whey protein | 3 scoops |
| Pumpkin pie spice | 1 tbsp |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tbsp |
| Salt | 1/4 tsp |
| Sweetener | 3 to 6 tbsp, to taste |

1. Blend everything in a blender until completely smooth. Whey and pumpkin both clump if only whisked. Make it slightly sweeter and spicier than you want, since freezing dulls flavor.
2. Divide evenly among pints, filling each to the MAX line. Top off with milk if short.
3. Freeze 24 hours on a flat, level surface.
4. Let the pint sit 5 to 10 minutes, then spin on **Lite Ice Cream**.
5. Add 1 tbsp milk and **Re-spin**. Repeat once more if still crumbly.

### Greek yogurt frozen yogurt

Thick, tangy, and about 30 g protein. Needs no stabilizer because the yogurt carries it.

- 1 1/4 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 tbsp honey or sweetener of choice
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt

Whisk until smooth, freeze, spin on **Lite Ice Cream**. Re-spin with 1 tbsp milk. Add fruit after the first spin with **Mix-in**.

### Cottage cheese chocolate

Sounds wrong, tastes like a rich chocolate soft serve. Blending is mandatory.

- 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder
- 3 tbsp sweetener of choice
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 tbsp sugar-free chocolate pudding mix

Blend everything in a blender until completely smooth. Freeze, spin on **Lite Ice Cream**, Re-spin.

### Protein powder base (no shake)

- 1 scoop (about 30 g) whey or whey-casein blend protein powder
- 1 1/2 cups milk of choice
- 1 tbsp sugar-free instant pudding mix
- Sweetener to taste

Whisk or blend until smooth. Casein blends give a noticeably creamier result than pure whey isolate. Spin on **Lite Ice Cream** and Re-spin.

## Sorbets (dairy-free)

Sorbet is the easiest category. Fruit plus sugar, nothing else required.

### Canned fruit sorbet

The one-ingredient trick. A single 15 oz can of fruit in syrup, poured into the pint with its syrup, frozen, and spun on **Sorbet**. Peaches, pears, pineapple, and mandarin oranges all work. Fruit in juice works but is icier; add 1 tbsp sugar.

### Mango

- 2 cups frozen mango chunks, thawed
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 cup water

Blend until smooth, freeze, spin on **Sorbet**. Re-spin if needed.

### Strawberry lemonade

- 2 cups strawberries
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 cup water

Blend, freeze, spin on **Sorbet**.

### Raspberry

- 2 cups raspberries
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 cup water

Blend, then push through a fine mesh strainer to remove seeds. Freeze, spin on **Sorbet**.

### Watermelon

- 2 1/2 cups seedless watermelon, cubed
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- Pinch of salt

Blend, freeze, spin on **Sorbet**. Very icy by nature. If it comes out loose and slushy, that is correct for watermelon.

## Dairy-free creamy bases

### Coconut vanilla

- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 1/4 cup sugar or maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt

Whisk well (warm the can slightly if the cream has separated). Freeze, spin on **Ice Cream**.

### Oat milk chocolate

- 1 1/2 cups full-fat oat milk (the "barista" or "extra creamy" kind)
- 3 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 tsp xanthan gum

Blend everything until smooth and slightly thickened. Freeze, spin on **Lite Ice Cream**, Re-spin.

### Banana "nice cream"

- 3 ripe bananas
- 1/2 cup almond milk
- 1 tbsp peanut butter (optional)
- 1 tsp vanilla

Blend, freeze, spin on **Lite Ice Cream**. Naturally sweet, no added sugar needed.

## Kid favorites

### Birthday cake

Make the vanilla base with 1/2 tsp butter extract or almond extract. After spinning, add 2 tbsp rainbow sprinkles and run **Mix-in**.

### Fruit yogurt pops

Blend 1 cup vanilla Greek yogurt with 1 cup berries and 2 tbsp honey. Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**. Scoop into popsicle molds and refreeze, or serve right away as soft serve.

### Cereal milk

Steep 1 cup of a sweet cereal (cornflakes, cinnamon squares, or fruit loops) in 1 1/2 cups whole milk for 20 minutes. Strain, whisk in 1 tbsp cream cheese and 1/4 cup sugar, top with heavy cream to the MAX line. Spin on **Ice Cream**.

### Milkshake

Fill the pint about 2/3 with scoops of already-made Creami ice cream (or store-bought), add 1/2 cup milk, and spin on **Milkshake**. No freezing needed.

## Gelato and egg bases

### Vanilla gelato

Richer and denser than ice cream. This base is cooked.

- 4 egg yolks
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- Pinch of salt

1. Whisk the yolks, sugar, vanilla, and salt in a small saucepan.
2. Whisk in the milk and cream. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it coats the back of a spoon (about 165°F). Do not boil.
3. Strain into the pint. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until cold before freezing 24 hours.
4. Spin on **Gelato**.

### Pistachio gelato

Make the vanilla gelato base and whisk 3 tbsp pistachio butter or paste into the warm custard. Mix-in: 2 tbsp chopped pistachios.

## Troubleshooting

- **Crumbly or powdery after spinning:** This is normal on the first pass. Add 1 tbsp liquid and Re-spin. For protein bases, two Re-spins is common.
- **Soupy or melted:** The pint was not fully frozen, or it sat out too long. Refreeze a few hours and try again. Also check for too much alcohol or sugar in the base.
- **Icy, grainy texture:** Not enough fat or sugar. Add 1 tbsp cream cheese or 1 tbsp pudding mix to the next batch. Sorbets are naturally a bit icy.
- **Machine stops mid-spin or sounds strained:** Pint was frozen tilted, or it is too hard. Let it sit 10 minutes and try again. Check that the pint surface is flat.
- **Lid pops up or base overflowed:** Overfilled past the MAX line. Liquid expands when frozen. Scrape level and spin.
- **Flavor is flat after freezing:** Cold dulls flavor. Make the base taste slightly too sweet and too strong at room temperature.

## Make-ahead and storage

- Prep 3 or 4 pints at once and keep them frozen. A spun pint can be refrozen and Re-spun later; add a splash of milk before the second Re-spin.
- Leftover spun ice cream stored in the pint should be smoothed flat before refreezing so it spins evenly next time.
- Bases keep in the fridge 2 days before freezing, which helps gelato and egg bases develop flavor.
